# Return Rate Benchmarks

Average return rates by category:

- **Apparel/fashion**: 20-30% (highest of any category, driven by fit and
  sizing issues)
- **Electronics**: 8-12%
- **Beauty**: 5-10%
- **Home goods**: 8-15%

A return rate that is rising while order volume is flat is often a
product quality or sizing/description-accuracy issue rather than a
customer behavior shift. Return rate should always be analyzed alongside
the reason codes (if captured) — "wrong size" versus "item not as
described" versus "changed mind" point to very different fixes.
