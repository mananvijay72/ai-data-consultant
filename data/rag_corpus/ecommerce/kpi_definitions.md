# E-commerce KPI Definitions

**Gross Merchandise Value (GMV)**: Total value of goods sold through the
platform before deducting fees, discounts, or returns.

**Average Order Value (AOV)**: Total revenue divided by number of orders.

**Conversion Rate**: Orders divided by site visits/sessions, expressed as
a percentage.

**Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: Total marketing/sales spend divided
by number of new customers acquired in the period.

**Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Predicted total net profit attributed
to a customer over their entire relationship with the business.

**Repeat Purchase Rate**: Percentage of customers who make more than one
purchase within a defined period (often 12 months).

**Cart Abandonment Rate**: Percentage of shopping carts created that do
not result in a completed purchase.

**Return Rate**: Percentage of orders (by value or units) that are
returned by customers.
