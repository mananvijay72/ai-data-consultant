# Seasonal Patterns in E-commerce

- **Q4 (Oct-Dec)**: Typically the strongest quarter across nearly all
  categories due to Black Friday/Cyber Monday and holiday gifting; can
  represent 25-40% of annual revenue for gift-heavy categories.
- **January**: Often sees a revenue dip alongside a spike in return rate
  from Q4 gift returns and exchanges.
- **Back-to-school (Jul-Aug)**: Strong for apparel, electronics, and home
  organization categories.
- **Summer (Jun-Aug)**: Can be soft for indoor/home goods but strong for
  outdoor, travel, and apparel categories.

When evaluating a month-over-month decline, always compare against the
same month in the prior year rather than the immediately preceding month,
since e-commerce is highly seasonal.
