# Pricing and Discounting Effects

Frequent, deep discounting (more than ~30% off list price on a regular
cadence) tends to train customers to wait for sales, which can suppress
full-price conversion over time and compress margins even as revenue
appears stable or grows. A healthy discount strategy generally keeps the
percentage of revenue sold at a discount below 30-40% of total revenue,
with the remainder at full or near-full price. A rising share of revenue
coming from discounted SKUs, without a corresponding increase in new
customer acquisition, is a signal of margin erosion rather than genuine
demand growth.
