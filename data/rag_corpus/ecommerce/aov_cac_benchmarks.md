# AOV and CAC Benchmarks

Average Order Value varies enormously by category:

- **Fashion/apparel**: $60-120
- **Beauty/cosmetics**: $40-80
- **Electronics**: $150-400
- **Home goods/furniture**: $200-600
- **Grocery/consumables**: $40-90

A healthy LTV:CAC ratio is generally considered to be **3:1 or higher** —
meaning a customer generates at least 3x the profit of what it cost to
acquire them. A ratio below 1:1 means the business loses money on every
new customer acquired, which is unsustainable without extremely high
repeat purchase behavior making up the difference over time.

CAC payback period (time for gross margin from a customer to cover their
acquisition cost) of under 12 months is generally considered healthy for
DTC e-commerce brands.
