# Conversion Rate Benchmarks

Average e-commerce conversion rates (site-wide, across devices) generally
fall between 1.5% and 3.5%, though this varies by traffic source and
category:

- **Organic/direct traffic**: typically converts higher (2-4%)
- **Paid social traffic**: often lower (0.5-1.5%) due to lower purchase
  intent at click time
- **Email/retargeting traffic**: often the highest converting channel
  (3-6%+), since it targets warm audiences

A conversion rate significantly below category benchmarks, combined with
otherwise healthy traffic volume, usually points to friction in the
checkout flow, pricing/shipping cost surprises at checkout, or a mismatch
between ad creative and landing page expectations.
