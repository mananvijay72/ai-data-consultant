# Churn and Retention Norms

Repeat purchase rate benchmarks vary by category:

- **Consumables/subscription-adjacent (coffee, supplements)**: 40-60%+
  within 12 months is achievable and expected
- **Fashion/apparel**: 20-35% is typical
- **Big-ticket/durable goods (furniture, electronics)**: often under 15%,
  since repurchase cycles are naturally long

A declining repeat purchase rate over consecutive quarters, even with
flat or growing new customer numbers, is a common early warning sign of
product or service quality issues, and tends to precede a revenue decline
by one to two quarters if unaddressed.
