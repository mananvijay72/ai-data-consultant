# Common E-commerce Review/Feedback Themes

Customer reviews for e-commerce products typically cluster into themes:

- **Sizing/fit accuracy** (especially apparel and footwear)
- **Product quality vs. price expectations**
- **Shipping speed and packaging condition on arrival**
- **Accuracy of product description/photos**
- **Customer service responsiveness for issues**

A specific product with a sudden increase in negative reviews focused on
a single theme (e.g., "arrived damaged") often points to a packaging or
fulfillment-partner issue rather than a product design problem, and is
usually fixable without a product change.
