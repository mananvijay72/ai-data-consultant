# Cart Abandonment

Average cart abandonment rate across e-commerce sits around 65-75%,
meaning it is normal — not a crisis — for the majority of carts to not
convert. The commonly cited top reasons, in rough order of frequency:

1. Unexpected extra costs at checkout (shipping, taxes, fees)
2. Being required to create an account to complete purchase
3. A checkout process that is too long or complicated
4. Lack of trust in providing payment information
5. Slow website performance during checkout

Abandonment rates meaningfully above 80% often indicate a checkout UX or
pricing-transparency problem rather than normal browsing behavior.
