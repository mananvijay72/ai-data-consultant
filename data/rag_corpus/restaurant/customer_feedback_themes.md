# Common Restaurant Customer Feedback Themes

When analyzing guest reviews, recurring themes typically cluster into:

- **Speed of service** (wait times for seating, food, or the check)
- **Portion size / value perception**
- **Food temperature and consistency**
- **Staff friendliness / attentiveness**
- **Cleanliness (restrooms, tables)**
- **Noise level**

A sudden increase in complaints about a single theme (e.g., portion size)
within a short window often correlates with a recent operational change:
a new head chef, a supplier switch, or a cost-cutting initiative. It is
worth cross-referencing the timing of a feedback theme shift against any
known menu or pricing changes.
