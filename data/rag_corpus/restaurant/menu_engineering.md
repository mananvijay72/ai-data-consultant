# Menu Engineering Basics

Menu items are typically classified into four categories based on
popularity (sales volume) and profitability (contribution margin):

- **Stars**: high popularity, high profitability — promote these.
- **Plow Horses**: high popularity, low profitability — consider a price
  increase or cost reduction, carefully, since they drive volume.
- **Puzzles**: low popularity, high profitability — reposition on the
  menu or bundle to increase visibility.
- **Dogs**: low popularity, low profitability — candidates for removal.

A menu where "Dogs" make up more than 20-25% of items is generally
considered overextended and a candidate for simplification.
