# Labor Cost Management

Labor cost percentage (labor cost / revenue) should generally sit between
28-35% for full-service restaurants and 25-30% for quick-service. Prime
cost — labor cost percentage plus food cost percentage combined — is the
single number most operators watch weekly. A prime cost above 65% is a
common warning threshold; above 70% is generally unsustainable for
long-term profitability without pricing or efficiency changes.

Overtime spikes are the most common hidden driver of labor cost creep —
they often don't show up in headline "hours scheduled" numbers but do
show up in total labor dollars.
