# Seasonal Patterns in Restaurant Sales

Restaurant revenue is rarely flat across the year. Common patterns:

- **Q4 (Oct-Dec)**: Often the strongest quarter for full-service and fine
  dining due to holiday parties and gifting; can be weaker for concepts
  that rely on outdoor seating.
- **January**: Frequently the weakest month industry-wide ("New Year"
  dieting behavior, post-holiday spending fatigue).
- **Summer (Jun-Aug)**: Strong for casual/family concepts and anything
  with patio seating; can be weak for downtown lunch-focused concepts if
  office occupancy drops.
- **Valentine's Day, Mother's Day**: Single-day spikes that can equal
  3-5x a normal day's covers for full-service restaurants.

When explaining a revenue dip, always check whether the same dip occurred
in the prior year before attributing it to an operational problem.
