# Impact of Online Delivery Platforms

Third-party delivery (DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub) typically carries
15-30% commission fees, which materially changes effective margin on
those orders compared to dine-in or direct pickup. A restaurant with
delivery representing more than 30-40% of total revenue should evaluate
whether menu pricing on delivery platforms has been adjusted to offset
commission costs — many operators fail to do this and unknowingly erode
margin as delivery mix grows.
