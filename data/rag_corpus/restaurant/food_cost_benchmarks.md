# Food Cost Percentage Benchmarks

Industry-wide, food cost percentage typically falls in these ranges:

- **Quick-service restaurants (QSR)**: 28-32%
- **Casual dining**: 28-35%
- **Fine dining**: 30-40% (higher due to premium ingredients, but offset
  by higher menu prices)
- **Pizza concepts**: 25-30% (dough and cheese have favorable margins)
- **Steakhouses**: 38-45% (protein-heavy menus carry inherently higher
  food cost)

A food cost percentage more than 3-4 points above the concept's typical
range for two or more consecutive months usually signals a problem:
portion control issues, vendor price increases not reflected in menu
pricing, waste, or theft. A sudden spike in a single month is more often
seasonal (produce prices) or a one-time large purchase.

**Rule of thumb**: every 1 percentage point improvement in food cost
percentage on $1M of food revenue is roughly $10,000 in additional annual
gross profit.
