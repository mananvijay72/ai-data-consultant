# Table Turnover Norms

Table turnover varies heavily by service style:

- **Fast casual / counter service**: 4-6 turns per table per meal period
- **Casual dining (sit-down)**: 1.5-2.5 turns per meal period
- **Fine dining**: 0.75-1.5 turns per meal period (longer dwell time is
  expected and often desired)
- **Breakfast/brunch service**: typically the highest turnover of the day
  due to shorter average dwell time

Turnover below 1.0 for a casual dining concept during peak hours (weekend
dinner) is a signal of either understaffing, slow kitchen throughput, or
insufficient demand — these have very different fixes and should not be
conflated. Turnover well above the norm for the concept can correlate
with declining guest satisfaction scores if guests report feeling rushed.
