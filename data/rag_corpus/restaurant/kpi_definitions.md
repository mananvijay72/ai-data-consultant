# Restaurant KPI Definitions

**Total Revenue**: Sum of all sale amounts (net of discounts) over the reporting period.

**Average Order Value (AOV)**: Total revenue divided by number of orders. For
full-service restaurants, a healthy AOV is typically 1.5-2x the average
menu item price.

**Food Cost Percentage**: (Cost of goods sold / Food revenue) x 100.
This is one of the most closely watched restaurant KPIs because it directly
drives gross margin.

**Table Turnover Rate**: Number of parties served per table in a given
period, divided by number of tables. Higher turnover means more revenue
per square foot, but too-high turnover can hurt guest experience.

**Labor Cost Percentage**: (Total labor cost / Total revenue) x 100.
Combined with food cost percentage, this forms the "prime cost," the
single most important profitability metric in restaurant operations.

**Same-Store Sales Growth**: Revenue growth compared to the same period
last year, for locations open in both periods. Isolates real growth from
growth due to new locations.
